CYBERSECURITY CHALLENGE

The Limitations of Conventional Phishing Detection

Why traditional static blacklists and black-box deep learning fail in modern perimeter defense:

CHALLENGE 01

Zero-Hour Ephemerality

Phishing campaigns exhibit an average lifespan under 4 hours. By the time a URL appears on central blacklists (DNSBL / Google Safe Browsing), initial victim credential theft is already complete.

CHALLENGE 02

The HTTPS Fallacy

Over 85% of modern phishing domains use valid Let's Encrypt TLS certificates. Green padlock browser indicators mislead users into falsely trusting transport encryption as authenticity.

CHALLENGE 03

Black-Box Opacity

Deep neural networks provide high accuracy but zero interpretability. SOC analysts cannot determine why a corporate domain was flagged, causing alert fatigue and discarded alerts.

STAGE 02 MATHEMATICS

Semi-Supervised Label Spreading on RBF Manifolds

Propagating categorical threat evidence through continuous feature space:

1. RBF Kernel Affinity: W_ij = exp(-γ · ||x_i - x_j||²), with diagonal W_ii = 0
2. Normalized Laplacian: S = D^(-1/2) · W · D^(-1/2)
3. Clamped Propagation: F_(t+1) = α · S · F_t + (1 - α) · Y
KEY PROPERTY

Cluster Manifold Assumption

Points residing in the same high-density topological neighborhood share label distributions. Graph propagation captures infrastructure clustering without deep training overhead.

KEY PROPERTY

Alpha Clamping Parameter

The clamping factor α = 0.20 retains original ground-truth seed labels while allowing soft evidence diffusion to unlabeled query URLs.

STAGE 03 SYNTHESIS

Zero-Order Takagi-Sugeno Fuzzy Logic Engine

Synthesizing manifold graph proximity and heuristic lexical risk into an interpretable verdict:

Antecedent Activation: w_k = μ_Graph(x_1) × μ_Lexical(x_2)
Composite Risk Index: Risk = (Σ w_k · z_k) / (Σ w_k)
RULE DESIGN

Monotonic Rule Matrix

9 expert-defined Sugeno rules guarantee monotonic behavior: increasing either graph proximity or lexical risk strictly increases or preserves composite risk.

AUDITABILITY

Crisp Contribution Breakdown

Every scan decomposes into exact rule activation percentages and crisp contribution scores (z_k), eliminating black-box uncertainty.

ORAL DEFENSE READINESS

Core Viva Defense Questions & Answers

Key technical distinctions frequently raised by project examiners:

Q1: WHY NOT DEEP GNNs?

LabelSpreading vs GNNs

GNNs require expensive GPU training, dense graph backpropagation, and large message-passing iterations. LabelSpreading operates deterministically on CPU in milliseconds via closed-form RBF Laplacian operations, ideal for real-time edge deployment.

Q2: WHY USE FUZZY LOGIC?

Fuzzy Logic vs Neural Output

Fuzzy inference maps continuous evidence onto linguistic uncertainty sets (Low, Med, High). It resolves conflicting inputs (e.g., brand-new host with clean lexical properties) without arbitrary threshold cutoffs.

Q3: WHAT IS HOMOPHILY?

Graph Homophily Proof

Our spectral analysis proves an 85%+ homophily index across the training manifold. Homophily is the core mathematical prerequisite for semi-supervised label diffusion.
